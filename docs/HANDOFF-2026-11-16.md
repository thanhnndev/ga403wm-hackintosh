## 5. Open items

### 5.1 StrixDgpuGate false trip (RTX powered this boot)
The gate reported "standard: loop guard (previous Eco boot did not finish)". The owner confirms no boot hung. The
guard: `strix-gpu-eco-try=1` in `start()` with a forced NVRAM sync, cleared by a thread call at 90 s; still 1 at the
next probe = stand down once. The previous Eco boot (11-11 22:38) ran 30+ min. Candidates: a silent early firmware
reset during the restart (two boots seen as one; the shutdown notes mention reset bit 23, "CPU shutdown event"), the
clear not persisting, or a second `start()`. NVRAM also holds stale `AAPL,PanicInfo000K..N` fragments. Now
`strix-gpu-eco-try` is 0 and `strix-gpu-mode` is `eco`, so the next boot should be Eco again. Installing gate
3FB90B94 (boot counter, cleared record, read-back, extra clears at 5 min and power-off) is how the next trip explains
itself; it needs the owner's OK and a reboot.

### 5.2 NMXGPU memory work (Steps 141-142): state and doubts
Files: `bsdcompat/src/nmx_page.c`, `xnu/nmx_mm.cpp`, `xnu/nmx_kext.cpp`, `patches/amdgpu-userptr-park.patch`,
`patches/ttm-pool-cap.patch` under `nmx-build-xkpi/src/gpu-port/xkpi`.

Two causes of wired memory in our layer: the 2 MB chunk pool never shrank (and TTM's pool was uncapped because xnu
never fires `vm_lowmem`), and an imported IOSurface stayed wired for the whole life of its GPU buffer.

| Build | Change | Result (Chrome 1080p60 maximised, drops / 60 s; out-22 = 7) |
|---|---|---|
| out-23 | Step141: per-chunk free lists, release above a 64 MB reserve; TTM pool cap 128 MB | NMXGPU tag 1818 MB vs 1482 in use (out-22: 3412 vs 1440). 21 drops: release/refill churn |
| out-26 | Step141b: release after 5 s unused, 256 MB reserve. Step142: park (unwire) IOSurface buffers idle 10 s | 666 MB unwired, 0 failures. 38 drops; with the park off 0 and 142 |
| out-24/25/27/28 | intermediate | build checks only, never installed |
| **out-29 (live)** | Step141c prefill thread call; Step141d/e capped 60 s + 10 min peak floor; Step142b park after 60 s | 0 and 10 drops (light load, no Docker, RTX powered); 1 chunk made in the GPU path all session |

Verified on out-29: the prefill covers allocation bursts (`nmx_page_chunks_inline` 1, also under Docker); park
re-wire PASS (13 min probe: parks +3141, unparks +1347, every fail counter 0, no GPU fault/timeout/reset lines,
WindowServer pid unchanged; video paused 75 s then resumed, and tab switch after 75 s idle, both clean; up to 2.6 GB
parked).

Not verified: Mission Control thumbnails by eye (owner check in section 6); video drops under Docker load, over a
120 s window, in Eco; long runs; sleep/wake with parked buffers.

Sysctls: `debug.nmx_pages_live`, `nmx_page_chunks`, `nmx_page_free`, `nmx_page_chunks_freed`,
`nmx_page_chunks_filled`, `nmx_page_chunks_inline`, `nmx_page_hwm`, `nmx_userwire_*`, `nmx_surfwire_*`,
`nmx_userwire_parked*`, `nmx_userwire_parks/unparks/unpark_fail`, `nmx_upin_*`, `nmx_park_*`,
`nmx_userptr_park_ms` (RW; 0 turns the park off live). Boot-arg `nmxpark=0` disables parking.
Probe: `tools/890m/park-probe.sh` (branch `claude/upbeat-colden-d050ad`).

**Doubts worth a fresh look before any out-30.** Five builds in one evening chased video drops that our own allocator
policy caused, judged by a YouTube page whose runs ranged from 0 to 142 drops under the same load. The pool now keeps
256 MB + up to 1 GB by design, so its saving is modest, while the park does the large saving. Questions: is a
shrinking pool worth its complexity; should chunk memory come from the kernel's page allocator or be pageable; can
xnu's memory-pressure notification drive release instead of timers; should surfaces of a live swap chain be exempt
from parking (idea, not built). Open suggestions from the video session's review are at the end of the trace.

  `scripts/macos-install-step130.sh <kext>` (relinks the AuxKC, needs a reboot).
- No address hooks, signature or vtable patches into our own drivers. Version gates cover all of Darwin 25.x.
- Audit every boot-arg against all kexts before adding it. Never `-nvoff` or `nullmoth-remove` for dGPU-off. Do not
  touch the `rhv_*` boot-args, pmset or SIP without the owner.
- Never launch Edge (Chrome only). Downloads need permission. Do not delete user data without asking.
